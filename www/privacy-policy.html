PocketKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Applies to the PocketKit Android app

The short version

PocketKit works on your phone. We do not run a server for it, we do not collect your data, we do not have accounts, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Everything you create in the app (notes, recordings, locked files, routes, settings and so on) is stored only on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app:

  • Your settings, pinned tools, collections, recently used tools, colour and theme choice, and the history of your recent calculator results (the last 200, which you can clear in each tool).
  • Content you create in tools: notes, to-do lists, reminders, alarms, saved routes and places, health logs, invoices, counters, game scores, recordings, photos you save, and similar.
  • Files and passwords you put in the File Locker and Password Vault. These are encrypted on your device with a key made from your password. We cannot recover them: if you forget the password they cannot be opened.

Data you export or share (for example a recording, a GPX route or an invoice) leaves the app only when you choose to share it, and goes to the app you pick.

PocketKit asks for a permission only when you open a tool that needs it. You can refuse or revoke any of them in Android settings; the other tools keep working.

App lock and sharing

  • App lock. If you turn on the app lock, a salted, one-way hash of your PIN is stored on your phone. The PIN itself is never stored or sent anywhere. We cannot recover a forgotten PIN.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you connect your Google account, PocketKit can save a backup of your settings and tool data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, and restore it later. This happens only after you connect and tap Back up (or, with Pro, switch on automatic backup). The backup goes straight from your phone to your Google Drive. We never receive it and cannot read it. PocketKit asks for Google's Drive app-data permission and your email address, only to show which account is connected. You can disconnect in the app or revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com. Backups stay in your Drive until you delete them there.
  • Backup file. The Backup button saves your settings and tool data to a file that you choose where to send. It does not contain your PIN, Pro status, recordings, locked files or Password Vault.
  • Share. The Share button sends the text on the screen to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you tap it and choose an app.

Services provided by Google

  • Google Play Billing. The optional one-time "PocketKit Pro" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that the purchase exists; we never see your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to the payment.
  • Speech to Text. On Android, the speech recognition used by this tool is provided by your phone's speech service (usually Google). Depending on your phone, the audio you speak may be processed by that service. Text to Speech uses the voices installed on your phone.

Trial codes

If you enter a trial code, it is checked on your device against a built-in list; the code is never sent anywhere. Only the fact that a trial is active and when it ends is stored on your device.

Children

PocketKit is a general-purpose utility app. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Security

Because data never leaves your phone, its safety depends mostly on your phone's own protection (screen lock, encryption and updates). Locked files and vault entries are additionally encrypted with AES-256 using a key derived from your password.

Your choices

  • Delete everything the app stores by clearing its data in Android settings or uninstalling it.
  • Withdraw any permission at any time in Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example a new tool that uses a new permission, this page is updated and the date at the top changes. The latest version is always available at the address shown in the app's Google Play listing.
