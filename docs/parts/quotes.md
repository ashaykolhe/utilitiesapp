## Real Quotes
- id: realquotes
- category: daily
- plan: free
- needs: storage
- what: A large collection of genuine motivational quotes from real people, always with the author (and the source where it is well documented). Shuffle with Next and Back (or swipe the card), pick a category, search quotes and authors, save favourites, get a quote of the day, copy, share as text, or share as a picture card with a choice of eight colour styles.
- test:
  1. Open Real Quotes: a quote with its author shows. Tap "Next quote" several times: quotes change and do not repeat until all in the category have been shown. Tap the back arrow: the previous quote returns.
  2. Swipe the card left and right: next and previous quote.
  3. Tap a category chip (for example Courage): only quotes of that category come up and the list below shows them; tap "All" to return.
  4. Type a word or an author's name in the search box: matching quotes are listed; tapping one shows it. Search for "zzzz": "No quote matches that."
  5. Tap the star: the quote is saved and the "Saved" chip count grows. Open the "Saved" chip: it lists your saved quotes. Tap the star again: it is removed. Leave and reopen the tool: saved quotes are still there.
  6. "Quote of the day" shows the same quote all day. "Copy" puts the quote and author on the clipboard.
  7. "Share text" opens the share sheet with the quote, the author and "via PocketKit".
  8. "Share as picture": a preview card appears. Tap the colour dots: the style changes. "Share picture" opens the share sheet with a square PNG; choose an app and check the picture shows the whole quote and the author. "Close" returns to the tool.
  9. A very long quote still fits on the picture (smaller text, nothing cut off).

## Goofy Quotes
- id: goofyquotes
- category: fun
- plan: free
- needs: storage
- what: Silly, original motivational quotes to make you smile, in categories such as work, food, fitness and Mondays, plus a Mixer that invents brand new ones from templates every time you tap it. Shuffle, search, save favourites (generated ones too), quote of the day, copy, share as text, or share as a picture card in eight colour styles.
- test:
  1. Open Goofy Quotes: a funny quote shows. "Next quote" shuffles without repeating until the category is used up; the back arrow and swiping the card go back and forward.
  2. Tap "Mix a brand new one" several times: each tap gives a different, grammatical sentence, labelled "Freshly mixed". Tap the star on one you like: it is saved and still shows (with the same words) under the "Saved" chip after you leave and reopen the tool.
  3. Category chips filter the quotes; the search box finds words in quotes; "No quote matches that." appears for nonsense.
  4. "Quote of the day", "Copy" and "Share text" work as in Real Quotes (no author line).
  5. "Share as picture": preview, eight colour styles, "Share picture" opens the share sheet with a square PNG that shows the whole quote and the "PocketKit · Goofy Quotes" footer.
  6. Everything is family friendly: no swearing and no insults in any quote or mixed sentence.
